Everything a home NAS needs

One place for your disks and files

Set up storage, share it with every device in your home and keep it backed up, without the command line.

RAID

RAID 0, 1, 5, 6 and 10 with hot spares, expansion, disk replacement and data scrub.

Disk health

S.M.A.R.T. status, temperatures and self-tests, with an alert before a disk fails.

Shared folders

SMB, NFS, WebDAV and Time Machine, with permissions per user, group and folder.

OneDrive backup

Backup or two-way sync in a short wizard, with every file verified after each run.

Recycle bin

Restore deleted files and earlier versions of a file for 30 days.

Scheduled scripts

Run backups and clean-ups on a schedule, with live output and progress.

Alerts

An email when a disk fails, a volume fills up or a backup goes wrong.

Activity log

Everything Talon and the system do in one searchable list, with a year of usage graphs.

Backup

To a USB or internal disk when you plug it in or every day, with earlier versions.

Antivirus

ClamAV scans of whole volumes, on demand or scheduled, with a quarantine.

Updates

Talon and the operating system update from the web interface, or automatically.

Phones and tablets

The whole interface works on a phone, in light and dark mode.

Warning

Use at your own risk. Talon is free software, provided as is, without any warranty. The authors are not responsible for any data loss.

Always keep a backup of your important files on another device or in the cloud. RAID is not a backup: it keeps the NAS running when a disk fails, but it does not protect against deleted files, ransomware, theft, fire or a failed NAS.
